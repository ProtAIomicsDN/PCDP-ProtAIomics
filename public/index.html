Final confirmation

Your download contains the Word PCDP and a structured JSON backup including publications, other outputs, dissemination and communication records. Keep the JSON for your next review; this is not a submission to the European Commission portal.

Your PCDP answers are processed in your browser to generate your Word document and JSON backup. The app does not send your PCDP narratives to an external writing service. If you actively register for email reminders, only your email address, network, review date and reminder preference are sent to the reminder service. This tool does not replace discussion with your supervisor(s) or formal doctoral monitoring.
